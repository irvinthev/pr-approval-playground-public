Assurance / proposed control library

From risk to independently testable evidence

Explore a proposed risk-domain catalog. Every control names a consequential transition, operational requirement, possible enforcement, expected evidence and an adverse challenge. This is a design exercise intended for feedback—not a certified framework or conclusion on any organization's operations.

Draft control architecture. The 10 catalog entries are illustrative design objectives, not ten implemented controls, exhaustive coverage, or verified SOX criteria. The seven domains are an initial organizing proposal informed by independent critiques; no framework or model has endorsed them. Source references are thematic unless specifically validated.

Assurance methods

Risk domain = organization. Protected transition = operational unit. Test method = basis for assessing a particular assertion.

Framework relationship and limits. Applicable ICFR/ITGC requirements must be evaluated using actual control narratives and relevant audit criteria. NIST AI RMF, NIST SSDF, CSA AICM, OWASP, ISO/IEC 42001 and engineering playbooks contribute different voluntary design and threat lenses. AICPA's proposed AI-related Trust Services Criteria remain proposals, not SOX criteria. This draft catalog deliberately does not assert clause-level equivalence or audit reliance.
Previous inventories. The earlier 14-objective, WEB15 and WB16 RCM sets used overlapping IDs with different meanings. This replacement is a new conceptual catalog, not a claim that those controls were reconciled or that their coverage was preserved. Review the historical reconciliation before treating this as a complete program library. The previous downloadable workbook is not superseded as an audited record.
