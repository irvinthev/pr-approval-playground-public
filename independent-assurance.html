    Traditional vs. automated-control testing

    Transaction-focused: Select relevant changes from an independently verified population and inspect authorization and supporting evidence.

    Enforcement-focused: Inspect access and configuration, reperform relevant positive and negative cases, corroborate production completeness, and consider evidence across the reliance period. Different controls may require a mix of both.

    RELATED CONTROL OBJECTIVES
    Cross-cutting risks: what would assurance need to establish? ▸

    These are testing lenses—not standalone findings or evidence that a control operates effectively.

    • Authorization / SoD: trace a complete PR-to-release example and inspect who can approve or change policy, bot identities, rulesets, and override rights.
    • Classification / AI reliability: test adjudicated unsafe and unknown-impact cases, disagreement, invalid outputs, and model or prompt changes.
    • Configuration integrity: inspect the history and effective authorization of changes to eligibility rules, required CI, model configuration and deployment gates.
    • Deployment integrity: reconcile approved revision, artifact digest and deployed revision; test manual and emergency paths.
    • Monitoring completeness: corroborate production populations independently, inspect exceptions and verify shutdown or rollback procedures.

    03 / EXTEND THE CONCLUSION

    One demonstration is a starting point

    A successful challenge shows what happened under the conditions tested. Reliance over a period needs evidence of changes, exceptions, coverage and continued operation.

    From a demonstration to evidence over time
    Testing lens: Distinguish design, implementation, operation over the period and evidence reliability. A configuration screenshot or one negative test does not automatically establish annual operating effectiveness.
    • Design: Would it address the risk?
    • Implementation: Is it configured and enforced?
    • Operation: Did it work throughout the period, including changes and exceptions?
    • Evidence: Can the population and records be independently corroborated?
    How much testing is enough?
    • One screenshot or negative test supports only the conditions tested.
    • Reduced repeated testing of stable deterministic controls needs supporting change controls and evidence of unchanged operation.
    • Unchanged rules do not prove unchanged AI behavior. Consider model, prompt, tool and vendor changes separately.
    • Set testing extent from risk, coverage, bypass privileges, changes and exceptions. There is no universal sample size.
