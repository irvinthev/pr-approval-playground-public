ASSURANCE · EVIDENCE & TESTING

Can we rely on AI-mediated approval?

Three questions establish the assurance approach: what must already work, what should we test, and what evidence supports a conclusion? These are proposed evaluation objectives—not proof that a control exists or operates.

01 · Foundation02 · Safeguards03 · Evidence & conclusion
01 / ESTABLISH THE FOUNDATION

What must already be working?

AI approval depends on established engineering and security controls. Validate these foundations first; don't assume that introducing a second AI reviewer creates them.

Protected repositories

Branch rules, required checks and bypass paths are effective.

Independent authorization

Contributors and privileged automation cannot authorize excluded changes.

Policy and administrator independence

Approval rules, models and privileged settings are independently governed.

Approved testing baseline

Required CI, security and business-critical tests are defined and enforced.

Security trust boundaries

Untrusted PR inputs cannot acquire inappropriate agent privileges.

Independent evidence

Approval, merge, bypass and deployment populations can be corroborated.

02 / EVALUATE THE SAFEGUARDS

What would we test?

Six objectives cover the primary assurance decision. The detailed testing procedures remain available in the reference material.

Assurance objectiveEvidence needed to assess it
Change eligibilityValidate repository exclusions, ownership and downstream dependency maps. Inject a hidden billing dependency and a missing map; verify mandatory human routing.
AI reliabilityIndependently adjudicate real and adversarial PR cases; measure consequential false-low-risk classifications and test two-model correlated misses.
AuthorizationInspect effective GitHub rulesets, bot privileges and CODEOWNER routes; test absent/skipped/stale AI outcomes and privileged bypass attempts.
Policy integrityTrace approved model, prompt, mapping and rule changes; attempt self-relaxing policy edits; verify current revision and policy version binding.
Deployment integrityReperform failed/skipped CI gates, source-to-artifact checks, and approval-to-production version/digest tracing.
Continuous monitoringReconcile complete independent merge/deploy populations to recorded approvals; test missing events, alerts, escalation and suspension.

Security is not a seventh isolated checklist: evaluate prompt injection, least privilege, secret handling and enforcement independence within the relevant authorization, testing and monitoring procedures.

03 / SUPPORT THE CONCLUSION

What does the evidence establish?

A plausible workflow, a passing demonstration and evidence of sustained control operation are three different conclusions.

Design

Are scope, authority, safeguards, ownership and failure responses appropriately specified?

Implementation

Are effective rules, permissions, checks and negative tests demonstrably configured?

Operation over time

Do complete, reliable records support consistent execution, exceptions, model changes and intervention across the relevant period?
