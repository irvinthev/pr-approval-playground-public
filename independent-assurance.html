      What might change in the audit approach?

      Traditional change testing often concentrates on individual approvals and supporting change records. DevOps shifts attention toward independently validating enforced policy, privileges, CI/CD configuration, bypass routes and source-to-production traceability. AI-mediated approval adds testing of classification boundaries, model and prompt changes, fail-safe behavior and bot authority.

      Potential benefit: Once automated controls and evidence are independently validated over the relevant period, Audit may be able to focus more on exceptions rather than repeating approval inspection for every selected change.

      Important limit: Missing deployment populations, untested bypass paths, unreliable AI classification or unresolved monitoring exceptions may increase—not reduce—direct testing. This is an assurance judgment, not an automatic SOX exemption.
