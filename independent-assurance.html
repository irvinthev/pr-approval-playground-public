    Traditional vs. automated-control testing

    Transaction-focused: Select relevant changes from an independently verified population and inspect authorization and supporting evidence.

    Enforcement-focused: Inspect access and configuration, reperform relevant positive and negative cases, corroborate production completeness, and consider evidence across the reliance period. Different controls may require a mix of both.

    RELATED CHAPTER 3 CONTROLS
    03 / TESTING SUFFICIENCY

    Before relying on automated evidence

    Design: Would the control prevent or detect the stated risk? Implementation: Is it configured and enforced? Operating effectiveness: Did it function throughout the relevant period, including changes and exceptions? Evidence reliability: Can the underlying population and data be independently corroborated?

    Configuration inspection plus one approved positive/negative test can be powerful evidence of point-in-time enforcement. It is not a universal substitute for period testing, bypass validation, or sample-based procedures where warranted.

    Illustrative, fictional audit approaches. Not prescribed testing procedures, a SOX effectiveness conclusion or a claim that any sample size is always sufficient.
