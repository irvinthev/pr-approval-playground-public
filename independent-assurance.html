The question isn't “sample 25 PRs or test one.” It's whether the control is appropriately designed, enforced, and operating—and what independent evidence is sufficient to support that conclusion.

03 / HOW MUCH TESTING IS ENOUGH?

“Test of one” is not a universal shortcut.

A point-in-time configuration inspection, combined with a positive and negative reperformance, may support whether a deterministic rule is designed and enforced at that point. It does not by itself establish that the rule remained effective throughout the period, that all routes to production were covered, or that every relevant AI classification was correct.

Audit may also need configuration-change history, access and bypass testing, independent population reconciliation, repeat testing over time, and transaction or exception testing. The mix depends on the control, degree of automation, risk, and reliance period.

Modernization opportunity: Shift repetitive transaction inspection toward testing enforcement and reliable exceptions where justified—not replacing a sample with one screenshot.
