01 / Plan

What problem are we solving?

Human review can prevent mistakes, but it can also become a queue of perfunctory approvals. The design question is not “Can AI click approve?”—it is which approach improves delivery while preserving meaningful authorization and review quality.

Engineering objective

Reduce time waiting for routine PR reviews while maintaining quality and clear accountability for production changes.

What we would need to establish

  • Which PRs wait longest, and why?
  • Which reviews catch substantive defects?
  • Which changes actually require human judgment?

Choose the operating model

The authority delegated to AI matters more than the presence of AI in the workflow.

Human fast-track: deterministic eligibility simplifies approval, while a person still authorizes the change.
