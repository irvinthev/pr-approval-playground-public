Core method

Authority matters more than agent count.

  1. ParticipationWhere AI assists or acts
  2. AuthorityWhat it can decide or execute
  3. ExposureWhat can go wrong and its impact
  4. SafeguardsWhat limits or detects failure
  5. ToleranceWhat management permits

AI that writes code, AI that recommends a review decision, and AI that authorizes a production change introduce different risks. More agents do not necessarily provide independent authorization or lower residual risk.

What this guide is—and is not

It offers an illustrative assessment method and proposed safeguards informed by secure-development, AI-risk, software-supply-chain and assurance principles. It does not prescribe an architecture, certify tools, establish SOX compliance, or demonstrate that any organization's controls operate effectively.

Risk ratings and automated authorization are not generated from an arbitrary score. Management establishes risk tolerance and eligibility boundaries; Engineering implements and enforces them; assurance evaluates the evidence.
