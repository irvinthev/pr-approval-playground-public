02 / Engineering implementation

What makes this workflow possible?

In this fictional automated-approval design, Engineering would need more than AI risk scores. Existing CI/CD and authorization controls may provide simpler alternatives; these are possible responsibilities, not a prescribed stack.

Policy & dependency engine

Evaluate scoped repositories, protected paths, service dependencies, and mandatory exclusions.

CI validation & code review

Run builds, tests, security checks, and applicable human review independently of AI risk scoring.

AI reviewer services

Invoke model reviewers on the same revision, record results, and handle uncertainty or disagreement.

Authorization service

Combine valid evidence with approved rules and restricted identities; invalidate stale approvals.

GitHub & deployment enforcement

Require protected merge conditions and verify build provenance and production release routes.

Observability & governance

Retain decision traces, monitor exceptions, version rules and models, and support suspension or rollback.
