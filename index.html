Approach under examination: two AI reviews, conditional automated approval

A developer submits a pull request. Two AI-based reviews assess the change, while deterministic eligibility checks identify protected files, known critical dependencies and exclusions; CI provides technical evidence. A proposed, separately governed authorization gate combines these inputs for the exact revision, routing eligible changes to automated approval and uncertain or excluded changes to human review or a block. Protected merge and deployment controls provide further enforcement.

Not a universal blueprint. Two AI assessments do not, by themselves, provide independent authorization or segregation of duties. This fictional design is one option to challenge alongside human review and simpler deterministic automation.
