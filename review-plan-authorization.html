Interactive challenge · Approval of record

A human approved the AI review plan. Did anyone approve the code?

An AI drafts a review plan for a fictional pull request. A human signs off on the plan. Agents then inspect the code, run tests, declare the plan passed, and may be instructed to approve the PR. Decide whether that chain actually provides valid change authorization.

The point is not that automation must always be forbidden. It is that planning oversight, delegated authority, and approval of a particular revision are different decisions.

Evidence to ask for

    The distinction to carry back to Engineering

    Plan approval endorses a review procedure. Delegation approval authorizes a defined mechanism and scope, subject to independently enforced conditions. Code approval is authorization of the actual revision by an appropriately authorized reviewer. None substitutes for required CI, GitHub controls, or a traceable build and release path.

    Why the extra agent-attestation step matters: the same agents may be doing the work, judging whether the work passed, and initiating an approval. Their assertion that a human-approved plan passed does not independently verify review quality, authorize this revision, or establish that privileged merge permissions are restricted.

    A policy may permit automated approval for eligible changes. The human does not necessarily need to review every line, but someone must own the delegation decision and its boundary. Two models agreeing, by themselves, do not create that authority.
