01 / General risks

What could go wrong across the workflow?

Unauthorized changes

A change reaches production without appropriate authorization.

Incorrect classification

A significant change is incorrectly deemed low risk.

Segregation of duties

An actor can influence mechanisms that authorize their own changes.

AI reliability

AI misses, disagrees or silently fails.

Configuration integrity

Rules, prompts or permissions change without control.

Deployment integrity

The shipped artifact differs from the approved revision.

Monitoring gaps

Production failures or bypasses are not detected.

CROSS-STAGE FAILURE

All checks passed. Was the change actually authorized?

A developer may propose changes to a policy engine, CI configuration, or release gate. If the same actor can also make those changes effective without independent enforcement, every downstream check could display green while the authorization mechanism has been weakened.

Key distinction: proposing a gate change is not the same as independently authorizing and deploying that gate change.
