Missing financial logic coverage

A billing boundary case passes standard unit tests and two AI reviews because the business-specific assertion was never defined.

Safeguard: Define risk-based mandatory financial calculation and reconciliation tests, including negative and boundary cases.

Evidence: applicability matrix, required-check configuration, test cases, observed failures and exceptions.

Engineering test requirements →

Security check bypassed

A critical SAST or dependency finding is present but the scan is advisory or the job is removed.

Safeguard: Protect scanner policies and enforce blocking results in the merge gate.

Evidence: actual required checks, scanner outputs, policy changes and rejected merges.

Required security gates →

Unsafe canary promotion

Error rate stays green while transaction totals diverge, or promotion continues after a failing canary signal.

Safeguard: Define technical and business-level promotion thresholds, containment, safe rollback and alternate release patterns.

Evidence: rollout policy, baseline, business reconciliation, promotion/stop decision and recovery logs.
