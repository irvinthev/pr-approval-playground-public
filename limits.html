Limits and claim strength

This is a public, generic field guide. This is not a prescribed architecture and not a new regulatory standard, official mapping, certification or conclusion of effectiveness. Nothing on this page establishes production control effectiveness, SOX compliance or external-auditor reliance. Proposed controls and fictional examples require environment-specific evaluation.

TagMeaning
StrongNative, directly relevant evidence supports the stated claim; not a conclusion of effectiveness.
QualifiedSupports a narrower or conditional claim. Proposed safeguards and fictional examples use this tag; it does not mean they were tested.
TentativePlausible but unverified. Validate before relying on it.
UnsupportedInsufficient evidence to assert the claim. Do not treat it as a control or decision basis.

The page-level Qualified tag applies to proposed controls, recommendations and fictional examples throughout that page. Strong, Tentative and Unsupported exceptions remain attached to the specific claim. Technical detail inherits its page’s tag unless explicitly marked otherwise. Recommendations describe proposed behavior. Example numbers and records are fictional unless an external source is explicitly cited. Unknown evidence is undetermined, not a favorable default.

Frameworks inform reasoning; they do not endorse this architecture. Earlier reference mappings and product snippets have not all been revalidated. Items awaiting validation are recorded in open validations. Exact product behavior, plan availability and settings must be tested in the target environment.

The four outcomes are eligible for conditional authorization, human authorization required, blocked and AI capability suspended. Eligibility is not authorization; authorization is not release eligibility; risk acceptance cannot waive mandatory safeguards.

Browser simulations and downloaded example records are editable educational material. They are not authentic evidence, live configuration, immutable logs or production enforcement. Advanced experiments retain historical models; they do not supersede the authority-first method.

Phase 1 changes content using the existing static architecture. Data-driven rendering, a documented private-overlay schema, runnable queries and exports remain deferred backlog work. The site currently serves no backend, accounts or external API calls.
