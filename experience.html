03 / Two perspectives

Good engineering. Built-in assurance.

⚙ Engineering

Accelerate routine reviews with reproducible checks, explicit policy boundaries, dependable deployment traces, and visible exceptions. Reduce friction without letting the classifier grant itself authority.

⌕ Independent assurance

Test whether the exact reviewed change was authorized and deployed, assess detector coverage and false approvals, and verify administrator independence and bypass routes. A clean AI score alone is not proof.
