03 / DESIGN · Engineering & Safeguards

What actually prevents an unsafe approval?

Engineering has multiple choices: improve human review, let agents execute explicit decisions, simplify the route, or delegate narrow approval authority. The right architecture depends on the problem and the independent enforcement.

The architectural choice

Who decides is not necessarily who clicks Approve. Who clicks Approve is not necessarily who controls credentials, branch rules, or deployment.

An LLM’s instruction to “only approve safe changes” is not an access-control boundary.

Where should authority sit?

Compare alternatives on decision rights, enforcement, developer friction and the failure each design must withstand.

DECISION TAKEAWAY

Improve workflow economics without confusing AI advice, human authorization, bot execution and policy delegation. Validate that enforcement holds across all usable paths, including MCP, CLI, API and privileged identities.

BUILD FOR A REAL THREAT MODEL

The boundary is broader than the Approve button.

A protected branch helps only if every effective route respects the policy. Credentials, ruleset exemptions, merge queues, rebase behavior, build provenance and deployments are all part of the effective pathway. The same authority and trust-boundary analysis applies to coding, testing and deployment agents, even when no GitHub approval is involved.
