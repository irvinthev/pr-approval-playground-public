What AI changes in software delivery

Not every risk is new. AI changes who produces code, who interprets it, how actions are executed, the variability of decisions, and the speed and scale at which mistakes propagate.

Nondeterminism does not mean uncontrolled authorization

Probabilistic judgment

An AI reviewer assesses the diff and may label it low-risk. Test behavior using labeled cases, repeated runs, critical miss analysis and configuration tracking.

Deterministic constraint

A trusted policy gate rejects excluded files or missing requirements regardless of the AI verdict. Challenge this boundary with deliberate bypass attempts.

Design principle: Let models provide judgment where useful; use independently enforced rules to bound what that judgment can authorize. Two agreeing models are not an independent hard gate.

Three scenarios, three different assurance questions

AI advisory review

A model comments on a pull request; a human still authorizes changes.

Failure: Incorrect guidance, over-reliance, context leakage and unobserved quality deterioration.

Design: Keep GitHub approval authority with people; evaluate useful findings and severe misses.

Challenge: Seed representative known defects, measure accepted findings and false negatives, test outbound context.

See Engineering + IA investigation →
Agent-executed human approval

An engineer decides; an agent uses GitHub tools to execute that decision.

Failure: Execution authority may exceed a one-PR, one-revision human instruction.

Design: Bind intent to PR/SHA; restrict tools, identities and revocation; independently enforce scope.

Challenge: Attempt approval of another PR or amended SHA using the same agent access.

See Engineering + IA investigation →
Autonomous AI approval

A model's risk judgment may influence approval authority or eligibility.

Failure: False-low-risk classification becomes an authorization error; privileged identities may bypass review.

Design: Deterministic exclusions and independent authorization gate; fail to human; monitor drift and escapes.

Challenge: Have a prohibited shared dependency classified low-risk; confirm GitHub/eligibility gate still blocks.
