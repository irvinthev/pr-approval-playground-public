05 · Assurance

What evidence would justify relying on an AI-enabled SDLC architecture?

A model verdict, recorded approval, or management assertion does not by itself establish effective technical enforcement. This page preserves the eight assurance objectives developed in the original Field Guide and relates them to the current 21-scenario model. The mappings below are illustrative and require source-level and implementation validation.

Eight existing assurance domains

Assurance objectives

AS-01 · Delegated AI authority and governanceWorked assurance guide · fictionalWho was actually authorized to make this decision—and who can change the boundary?
Illustrative situation

AI approval is described as limited to documentation changes, yet an administrator can expand eligibility without independent authorization.

MANAGEMENT ASSERTION

“Only approved low-risk changes can receive automated authorization, and policy changes are independently governed.”

INDEPENDENT ASSURANCE OBJECTIVE

AI decisions and actions remain within a documented, appropriately approved business and technical boundary.

PRACTICAL TEST / CHALLENGE

  1. Trace an actual use case from business objective to owner, authorized actions, prohibited actions and approved scope.
  2. Compare the approved policy and decision rights with effective repository rulesets, service credentials and administration permissions.
  3. Attempt an excluded or unauthorized change, and review who can change eligibility or bypass restrictions.

EVIDENCE TO REQUEST

Approved operating scope, risk treatment record, versioned policy and change history, actual permissions, ruleset/bypass configuration, exception authorizations and test results.

POPULATION & RELIABILITY CHECK

Determine the full set of agents, repositories and eligible routes; reconcile ownership and policy versions to configuration independently obtained from the platform.

WHAT WOULD CONTRADICT THE CLAIM?

An unauthorized actor can alter the rule or approve an excluded change, or management cannot establish who owns the decision.

LIMIT OF RELIANCE

A governance approval alone supports intended authority, not proof of independent technical enforcement or sustained operation.

AS-02 · Agent identity, tools and untrusted inputsWorked assurance guide · fictionalCould the agent do something its instructions explicitly prohibit?
Illustrative situation

A malicious PR comment instructs a review agent to use GitHub MCP to post an approval, even though approval was not authorized.

MANAGEMENT ASSERTION

“The agent cannot approve, merge, exfiltrate data or modify policy outside its approved tool scope.”

INDEPENDENT ASSURANCE OBJECTIVE

Agent actions are attributable and restricted at enforceable tool, API, identity and trust boundaries, including during prompt-injection attempts.

PRACTICAL TEST / CHALLENGE

  1. Enumerate all service identities, tokens, tool connectors and available actions—including CLI/API paths outside MCP.
  2. Inspect effective privileges, token issuance, runtime isolation, rotation and privilege-change administration.
  3. Run a controlled prompt-injection and unauthorized tool-call challenge. Capture whether the platform rejects the attempted action.

EVIDENCE TO REQUEST

IAM and GitHub permission exports, token/OIDC configuration, tool manifests, gateway policies, rejected invocation traces, secret-access records and relevant log integrity settings.

POPULATION & RELIABILITY CHECK

Corroborate tool inventories and permissions from the actual platforms; do not rely exclusively on the agent’s configured prompt or self-reported tool list.

WHAT WOULD CONTRADICT THE CLAIM?

The agent or credential successfully performs a prohibited action, or a parallel API/CLI channel bypasses the expected restriction.

LIMIT OF RELIANCE

An agent output saying “blocked” is not evidence that the independent tool or API boundary rejected execution.

AS-03 · AI-authored code, tests and dependenciesWorked assurance guide · fictionalWhat if the code and its tests agree on the wrong answer?
Illustrative situation

AI changes a payment rounding function and modifies tests to reflect the incorrect rounding, producing green checks for the wrong business outcome.

MANAGEMENT ASSERTION

“Generated changes satisfy independently defined security, quality and critical business behavior requirements.”

INDEPENDENT ASSURANCE OBJECTIVE

AI-generated changes receive verification proportional to affected logic and cannot silently weaken acceptance criteria or introduce untrusted executable dependencies.

PRACTICAL TEST / CHALLENGE

  1. Identify high-impact generated code and affected business invariants independently of the model output.
  2. Inspect ownership and provenance of critical assertions, dependency manifests, lockfiles and protected test configuration.
  3. Introduce a seeded logic defect or untrusted dependency in a sandbox; test whether independently maintained checks detect or reject it.

EVIDENCE TO REQUEST

Commit diffs, requirements and invariant coverage, independent test results, SAST/SCA reports, dependency provenance, workflow permissions and exception records.

POPULATION & RELIABILITY CHECK

Validate the full change population, including generated files, transitive dependencies and changes to tests or quality gates—not only application source.

WHAT WOULD CONTRADICT THE CLAIM?

A seeded critical defect passes the required independent validation, or an unapproved package executes in the build environment.

LIMIT OF RELIANCE

Neither successful compilation nor a single passing test suite establishes the safety of all AI-authored changes.

AS-04 · AI review accuracy and risk classificationWorked assurance guide · fictionalWhat happens when both reviewers confidently miss the important change?
Illustrative situation

A small shared-library change appears low impact to both AI reviewers although a missing dependency reaches financially relevant processing.

MANAGEMENT ASSERTION

“AI reviewers reliably identify changes outside their eligible scope, and uncertainty routes to the proper authorization path.”

INDEPENDENT ASSURANCE OBJECTIVE

AI recommendations or low-risk classifications are sufficiently evaluated for their assigned use and cannot independently expand delegated authorization.

PRACTICAL TEST / CHALLENGE

  1. Construct an independently adjudicated test corpus with routine, high-consequence, adversarial and unknown-dependency cases.
  2. Evaluate errors by severity, false-low-risk rate, disagreement, correlated misses and version/prompt changes; repeat where nondeterminism matters.
  3. Prove a deliberately hidden critical dependency cannot pass the final independently enforced eligibility/approval path.

EVIDENCE TO REQUEST

Versioned model and prompt/config identifiers, adjudicated cases, evaluation results, classifier inputs, independent dependency coverage, policy/check executions and rejected merges.

POPULATION & RELIABILITY CHECK

Verify the evaluation set represents relevant repositories, change types, dependencies, edge cases and the actual operating model—not only convenient examples.

WHAT WOULD CONTRADICT THE CLAIM?

An ineligible dependency is classified as low risk and permitted to proceed, or correlated critical misses are not surfaced in acceptance criteria.

LIMIT OF RELIANCE

Model agreement, a small benchmark or a shadow score does not establish valid authorization or future reviewer reliability.

AS-05 · Approval, revision, merge and production lineageWorked assurance guide · fictionalDid the deployed artifact come from the exact revision that was authorized?
Illustrative situation

Revision A is reviewed; a rebase or generated file produces B; B reaches production while the recorded approval only covers A.

MANAGEMENT ASSERTION

“Only authorized, validated revisions can merge and reach production, including through alternate or emergency paths.”

INDEPENDENT ASSURANCE OBJECTIVE

Each production change is traceable to effective authorization for the actual merged revision and deployed artifact across every release path.

PRACTICAL TEST / CHALLENGE

  1. Trace one PR and exact SHAs through policy evaluation, review, approval, merge/queue, build digest, deployment and runtime version.
  2. Approve SHA A, change to B, then attempt merging or deploying B with the stale approval.
  3. Test manual, privileged, emergency and agent-driven release routes against effective restrictions.

EVIDENCE TO REQUEST

Git events and review state, effective rulesets and bypass actors, CI check-run SHAs, build provenance/artifact digests, deployment platform records and runtime identifiers.

POPULATION & RELIABILITY CHECK

Obtain independent deployment/production-version records and reconcile them to Git and CI populations, including nonstandard releases and direct changes.

WHAT WOULD CONTRADICT THE CLAIM?

A stale approval satisfies merge authorization, or an artifact without required lineage reaches production via another route.

LIMIT OF RELIANCE

A PR approval screenshot or WORM log alone cannot establish that the deployed content was authorized.

AS-06 · Data boundaries, providers and persistent knowledgeWorked assurance guide · fictionalWhere can sensitive data go—and what can an agent remember?
Illustrative situation

A coding agent reads sensitive configuration, sends it to an unapproved tool endpoint, and saves a poisoned RAG snippet as future guidance.

MANAGEMENT ASSERTION

“The AI workflow only accesses, transmits, stores and recalls information within defined approved boundaries.”

INDEPENDENT ASSURANCE OBJECTIVE

Code, secrets, customer information, retrieval content and saved agent memory are used and retained only within approved boundaries.

PRACTICAL TEST / CHALLENGE

  1. Map actual prompt, telemetry, tool, RAG and memory data flows, including vendor processing/retention boundaries.
  2. Use marked synthetic secrets or poisoned test content to challenge egress, retrieval trust and durable-memory write restrictions.
  3. Inspect data access, provider configuration, retention policy, revocation and memory deletion/rollback capability.

EVIDENCE TO REQUEST

Approved data classification and flow map, provider settings and terms, tool permissions, egress/DLP logs, retrieval provenance, memory write logs and deletion tests.

POPULATION & RELIABILITY CHECK

Check tool/platform egress independently; corroborate memory and retrieval stores, not just application request logs.

WHAT WOULD CONTRADICT THE CLAIM?

Marked sensitive data leaves the approved boundary, or untrusted content is promoted into durable agent guidance without required checks.

LIMIT OF RELIANCE

A vendor assurance report or “no training” setting alone does not prove actual runtime data handling or all tool paths.

AS-07 · Monitoring, incidents and agent lifecycleWorked assurance guide · fictionalWhen we say 'stop the agent,' does its actual access stop?
Illustrative situation

An agent is suspended after suspicious GitHub actions, but its token still works and pending jobs continue executing.

MANAGEMENT ASSERTION

“We can detect material deviations, stop the relevant authority and reconstruct affected actions.”

INDEPENDENT ASSURANCE OBJECTIVE

Material failures are detectable, owned, investigated and containable, including drift, automation misuse and agent retirement.

PRACTICAL TEST / CHALLENGE

  1. Trace a simulated unauthorized action or model/policy drift from signal creation to investigation, decision and response ownership.
  2. Attempt the documented stop mechanism, then independently verify token, job, tool and deployment access actually cease.
  3. Inspect model/policy updates, incident follow-up, credential revocation and decommissioning procedures.

EVIDENCE TO REQUEST

Alert rules and delivery, response runbooks, on-call records, policy/version events, suspension logs, revoked credential state, pending-job inventory and recovery tests.

POPULATION & RELIABILITY CHECK

Compare monitoring feeds against independent source and production events, including missed, delayed or suppressed events.

WHAT WOULD CONTRADICT THE CLAIM?

Unsafe actions continue after a declared stop or significant changes lack alerts and responsible investigation.

LIMIT OF RELIANCE

A configured alert or green dashboard is not evidence of complete monitoring coverage or successful containment.

AS-08 · Evidence reliability and complete populationsWorked assurance guide · fictionalHow do we know the events missing from the logs aren't the ones that matter?
Illustrative situation

Twenty-four of twenty-four GitHub-tracked deployments reconcile, while an independent production source shows twenty-five actual deployments.

MANAGEMENT ASSERTION

“Every in-scope production change has a reliable authorization and release evidence chain.”

INDEPENDENT ASSURANCE OBJECTIVE

Claims about AI-enabled change controls rely on complete, accurate, sufficiently independent records that can be reconstructed for the relevant period.

PRACTICAL TEST / CHALLENGE

  1. Define the period, systems and all in-scope source/change/release paths before selecting samples.
  2. Extract production events independently and reconcile to Git, CI, authorization and audit trail populations; investigate missing or duplicate records.
  3. Inspect event origin, timestamp, privileges, integrity controls and whether success assertions are computed from actual control results.

EVIDENCE TO REQUEST

Source-controlled population logic, independent production inventory, Git and CI exports, event schemas, account privileges, retained manifests, exceptions and reconciliation investigation.

POPULATION & RELIABILITY CHECK

Reconcile both directions, investigate unmatched records, and validate source-query parameters and evidence generation logic.

WHAT WOULD CONTRADICT THE CLAIM?

An untracked deployment or a hard-coded “passed” field exists while the relevant control did not execute successfully.

LIMIT OF RELIANCE

Complete records for the events you know about cannot prove completeness of the real-world population without an independent source.
