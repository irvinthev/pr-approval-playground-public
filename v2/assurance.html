05 · Assurance

What evidence would justify relying on an AI-enabled SDLC architecture?

A model verdict, recorded approval, or management assertion does not by itself establish effective technical enforcement. This page preserves the eight assurance objectives developed in the original Field Guide and relates them to the current 21-scenario model. The mappings below are illustrative and require source-level and implementation validation.

Move from claim to supported conclusion

01 · Authority

What was authorized?

Trace who granted authority, for which action and scope, and what changes require renewed authorization.

02 · Enforcement

What actually restricts actions?

Inspect effective identities, permissions, independent gates, tools, and bypass routes.

03 · Evidence

Can we reconstruct events?

Reconcile agent claims with platform records, failed actions, version changes and production outcomes.

AS-01 · Delegated AI authority and governance

What must be established: AI decisions and actions remain within a documented, appropriately approved business and technical boundary.
