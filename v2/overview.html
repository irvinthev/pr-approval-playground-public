What the research does—and does not—establish

Standards and threat research describe risks or principles; vendor documents describe platform features. Neither verifies a company's actual identities, permissions, rulesets, tests, or outcomes. The Field Guide's prioritization and engineering challenges are our own editorial model, not a validated quantitative rating.

Primary sources: NIST SSDF, NIST AI RMF, OWASP GenAI Security, CoSAI, Google SAIF, COSO, and vendor documentation for GitHub, Anthropic, Cursor, and OpenAI.

Publication note: A downloadable research crosswalk will be added after source-level verification and a final review. No unpublished working register is offered for public download.
