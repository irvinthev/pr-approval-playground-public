05 / SUBSTANTIATE · Independent Assurance

What can the evidence actually establish?

AI changes how code is generated, reviewed, authorized, and released—and exposes weaknesses in assurance that existed before AI. Begin with a testable claim, then choose evidence capable of showing what the delivery system actually did.

The assurance boundary

Management designs, operates and monitors the system. Independent Assurance evaluates the claims—not merely whether a policy document or dashboard exists.

A model score cannot certify effective control operation. Nor does a human attribution automatically prove human judgment.

FIVE COMPLEMENTARY TEST LENSES

Choose what you intend to prove.

Compare configuration, population, negative, judgment, and period-of-reliance testing. Each supports a different claim; no single method proves every approval, model decision, or production pathway is safe.

DECISION TAKEAWAY

A defensible conclusion is scoped to the assertion, system boundary, evidence provenance, tested conditions and unresolved exceptions. “Not established” is preferable to a polished but unsupported green status.

EVIDENCE BY DESIGN

Make credible evidence a by-product of engineering.

Engineering can link the authorized decision, exact revision, tool action, enforced gate, build identity, artifact and deployment into provenance-backed records. Deterministic reconciliation should establish what matches and what remains unresolved before AI helps investigate exceptions. An independent assessor must still verify source completeness, integrity, alternate paths, and the claim being made; an agent-generated trace is not automatically independent evidence.
