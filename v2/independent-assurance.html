05 / SUBSTANTIATE · Independent Assurance

What can the evidence actually establish?

A sampled GitHub approval, a reconciled population and an adversarial test answer different questions. The stronger assessment begins with a precise claim, then selects the evidence that can support it.

The assurance boundary

Management designs, operates and monitors the system. Independent Assurance evaluates the claims—not merely whether a policy document or dashboard exists.

A model score cannot certify effective control operation. Nor does a human attribution automatically prove human judgment.

CLAIM / METHOD / LIMIT

Choose what you intend to prove.

Compare the strength and limits of three methods. No single method independently establishes full authorization, accuracy and release integrity.

DECISION TAKEAWAY

A defensible conclusion is scoped to the assertion, system boundary, evidence provenance, tested conditions and unresolved exceptions. “Not established” is preferable to a polished but unsupported green status.

EVIDENCE BY DESIGN

Ask what the record cannot show.

Link the authorized decision to the exact revision, actual tool execution, enforced GitHub rules, merge path and deployed artifact. Test negative routes and source-population completeness before using automation to reduce audit effort. For code-generating and test-writing agents, quality, security and tool permissions require additional evidence beyond approval records.
