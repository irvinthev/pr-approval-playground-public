What's different from a legacy ITGC RCM?

Shift from prescribing a specific sequence of manual tickets, reviews and CI tools to defining enforceable outcomes: authorized changes, protected policy, dependable tests, constrained automation, traceable artifacts and complete deployment paths.

One technical mechanism may satisfy several objectives. Conversely, a green check may not satisfy even one objective if its authority, completeness or operating boundary cannot be established.

Where does SOX fit?

SOX relevance is determined by whether changes affect financial-reporting processes or supporting controls—not the presence of AI alone. For financially relevant changes, management must establish an appropriate authorization mechanism and dependable evidence. External auditor reliance is a separate assessment.

AI model scoring, repository scoping and source-to-production provenance each answer different questions. None independently establish change authorization effectiveness.
